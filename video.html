    The pipeline

    From brief to proof of play

    Nothing runs without consent

    The location owner approves every campaign, can block entire categories and keeps protected slots for building notices.

    Nothing plays without a record

    Each device logs scheduled versus actual plays, completion, uptime and errors — the proof-of-play report an advertiser can audit.

    Nothing is claimed without a label

    Verified delivery, modelled exposure and measured engagement are reported separately, never blended into a single "views" number.

    Chapter 3

    What a DSP actually is

    A demand-side platform is the buying cockpit: an advertiser or agency sets budget, audience and goals, and the DSP decides which impressions to buy across many publishers and formats — display, video, CTV, audio and digital out-of-home. The Trade Desk is the reference independent DSP: it does not own inventory, so optimisation is not tilted towards its own media.

    • Who buysBrands and agencies
    • What it optimisesAudience and outcome
    • InventoryBorrowed, not owned
