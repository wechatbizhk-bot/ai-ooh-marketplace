    Proprietary inventory

    Hologram devices need different creative rules

    Rotating LED hologram fans are pseudo-volumetric displays: the background must be pure black, the subject must stay inside the safe area, and small text simply will not resolve. The studio checks all of this automatically and converts ordinary artwork into device-compatible animation.
