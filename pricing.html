Pricing & commercial models

Priced like outdoor media, booked like a hotel room

The marketplace supports several commercial models. The MVP runs on fixed daily/weekly pricing plus share of voice; cost-per-impression, cost-per-scan and real-time bidding come later.

Rate card

Device day rates and venue multipliers

A location's list price is the sum of its device day rates multiplied by the venue-type multiplier. Landlord floor prices always override a discount.

Reference hardware prices (from the product FAQ) are HK$1,280 for 3DNE-P30S and HK$6,330 for 3DNE-P65YD. Whether the platform, the landlord or a partner owns the device changes the revenue share, not the advertiser price.

Ways to pay

Cards, PayPal, crypto or local Hong Kong rails

The provider fee is charged on top of media value and never deducted from the landlord share, so the split you model above stays intact.

Fees shown are the provider's published rates and are configurable in assets/js/config.js. Crypto is quoted with a 15-minute rate lock and settled to HKD on confirmation.

Pricing is quoted in HK$ on the platform. Thailand campaigns show an indicative ฿ equivalent at the configured rate and settle in THB.

Revenue sharing

Decide where the money goes

Move the sliders to model a landlord contract. The allocation must total 100%; payment processing is deducted separately at cost.

—
The default assumes the platform supplies the device free of charge and keeps the media and technology fee. If the landlord buys the device, shift the landlord share up and the platform share down.

Payment processing is charged to the advertiser on top of the media value, so the allocation percentages apply to the full HK$10,000.
